About the data

Everything here is read from game files extracted by hand. It is not taken from other fan sites and has not been checked against Project Azoth, which may differ from the official game.

What to keep in mind

  • Gathering shows which nodes can drop a material. The files do not include drop chances or amounts.
  • No map locations yet. The files have no coordinates.
  • One recipe per item. Where several recipes make the same item, the standard (non-random) one is used.
  • Some names show as raw ids (about 300 categories) because the game files have no display name for them.
  • Stations: "Forge T2" means a tier 2 Forge or better.

Your data

Your craft list, prices and choices are saved in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
